How it works

1

Describe it

Write what you want in English or Arabic — a pen holder shaped like a castle tower, a chess knight, an owl figurine. The model appears in seconds and you can rotate it, resize it and pick a colour.

2

See the price

One final price, in your currency, including delivery to your country. It updates live as you change the size, material or colour.

3

Printed and shipped

After you order, your item is printed to order and shipped to you. Follow every step from your orders page until it arrives.

Made to last

  • Walls at least 2 mm thick and a solid internal structure, checked on every model before it is printed
  • Rounded edges, compact shapes — no thin spikes that snap
  • If a design would be fragile at the size you chose, the app tells you before you order
  • Sizes up to 25 cm
Not for food or drink. Printed items are not food-safe. Do not microwave, bake, or dishwash. Keep away from open flame.

Installing on Windows

  1. Click Install for Windows and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open Prompt2Print from your desktop.
